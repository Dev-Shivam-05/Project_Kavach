# ADR-017 — LiveKit Cloud for the SFU, with insertable-stream E2EE

Status: Accepted · 2026-08-04 · **not implemented** — no media plane exists in this build

## Context

Live audio and video during an incident (L5 of the degradation ladder, docs/02
§4.4) needs a selective forwarding unit and TURN relays that work across Indian
mobile carriers and CGNAT. Running that stack reliably is a specialist,
full-time job, and it is the kind of infrastructure whose failures appear only
under the exact conditions an emergency creates (PRD §4.5).

## Decision

LiveKit Cloud provides the SFU and TURN. The media itself is encrypted with
**insertable streams** so the SFU forwards ciphertext it cannot decode: the
provider is trusted for availability and routing, never for content.

## Alternatives

**Self-hosted SFU plus coturn** — rejected. It is the option that gives full
control and it is also the option that competes for the same hours as the
Phase 1 gate.

## Consequences

- A cloud vendor sits on the media path. Their outage is our outage, and their
  pricing is our pricing. This is tolerable only because media is L5: everything
  below it — the alarm, the SMS, the coordinates, the fan-out, the 112 handoff —
  works with no SFU at all.
- Insertable-stream E2EE is the whole basis for accepting a third party here. If
  it is ever disabled "temporarily for debugging", the decision is void and the
  provider becomes able to watch a family's emergencies.
- The audio buffer's legal position is **self-recording by the subject** and must
  stay that way (PRD §20.2). A guardian must never be able to enable another
  member's buffer, and a two-way audio feature makes that boundary easier to blur
  than the black box does.
- Duress incidents permanently disable two-way audio (FR-003, P-011). Whatever
  media plane arrives has to honour that at the state machine, not the UI.
- **Nothing is implemented.** There is no LiveKit dependency, no SFU client and
  no media transport anywhere in this repository. Two-way audio exists only as a
  policy flag (`audioByDefault` in `mobile/src/core/policy.ts`), and the only
  audio that exists is the black box's own local ring buffer. The decision is
  recorded; it is unverified, and its cost estimates are untested.

## Implementation

Not implemented. What exists is the boundary it will have to fit inside:

- `mobile/src/core/policy.ts` — `audioByDefault`, per scenario
- `mobile/src/t0/blackbox.ts` — the local, sealed, self-recorded buffer
- `docs/02-System-Architecture.md` §2.3.2 — the black box's legal footing
- PRD §20.2 — why self-recording is a materially different legal position
